1. Introduction

Welcome to Al-Taif (الطيف), a physics learning app for Grade 11 and Grade 12 students in Oman, developed by SmartHorizon ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our app works.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What information we collect (very minimal)
  • How we use that information
  • Your rights and choices
  • How third-party services we use handle data

By using Al-Taif, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Key Point: Al-Taif does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information that identifies you. Your study data is stored locally on your device.

2.1 Information You Provide

We do NOT collect:

  • ❌ Name
  • ❌ Email address
  • ❌ Phone number
  • ❌ User accounts or passwords
  • ❌ Social media profiles

Exception - Contact Us form: If you choose to reach out to us through the in-app "Contact Us" form or by emailing us directly, we receive whatever you include there - typically your name, email address, and message - solely to respond to your inquiry. This only happens if you initiate contact.

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Your lesson progress (reading time, video watch time, questions attempted)
  • Bookmarked questions and any notes you write on them
  • Handwritten annotations and whiteboard drawings you make on lesson materials
  • Practice exam sessions and results you've completed
  • Study streak and daily activity history
  • App settings and preferences (theme, font size, notification time, and similar)
  • Downloaded lesson files (PDFs, videos) you've chosen to save for offline use

2.2 AI-Assisted Grading (Optional Feature)

Al-Taif includes an option to use an artificial intelligence (AI) service to help evaluate written and justification-style exam answers, as an alternative to comparing your answer against the official model answer yourself.

If you use this feature:

  • The question text, the official model answer, and the text of your written answer are sent to a third-party AI service to generate feedback
  • No name, email, or other information that identifies you is included in this data
  • The AI service processes this content only to generate grading feedback and does not use it to identify you personally

Your control: This is a supplementary study aid, not your only way to check your answers - you can always compare your answer against the official model answer PDF instead.

2.3 Subscription Information

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When you subscribe to a semester's content, RevenueCat collects:

  • Anonymous user ID (generated by RevenueCat)
  • Subscription status and purchase events
  • Device type and OS version
  • App version
  • Country/region (from App Store or Google Play Store)

Payment Information: We never see or store your payment details. All payments are processed by Apple (via App Store) or Google (via Play Store). Your credit card and billing information is handled entirely by them according to their privacy policies.

Purpose: To verify subscription status and provide access to the content you've purchased.

2.4 Analytics and Performance Monitoring

Al-Taif uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring (both provided by Google) to understand how the app is used and to find and fix performance issues.

Firebase Analytics collects:

  • App usage events - for example, which lessons, videos, or PDFs you open, questions you answer (and whether correctly), exams you complete and your score, bookmarks you add, downloads, and searches you perform
  • Your selected grade level, as a general usage property (not tied to your identity)
  • Standard device and app diagnostic data collected automatically by Firebase (device model, OS version, app version, country/region, and an anonymous installation identifier)

Firebase Performance Monitoring collects:

  • Technical performance data such as app start time, screen rendering time, and network request duration - to help us find and fix slow or broken parts of the app

Neither service collects your name, email, or any other information that identifies you personally. Both are provided by Google - see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase's data processing and security terms.

4. Data Storage and Backup

4.1 Local Storage

By default, all your study data is stored only on your device. We do not have access to this data.

4.2 Optional Google Drive Backup

You can optionally back up part of your study data to your own Google Drive:

  • This feature is off by default - you must turn it on and sign in with a Google account to use it
  • We request access only to a private, hidden storage area of your Google Drive reserved for this app (Google's "app data" folder) - we cannot see, browse, or access any of your other Drive files
  • We store only your Google account's email address on your device, to show you which account is signed in

What gets backed up:

  • Lesson progress and bookmarks
  • Your study streak and activity history

Handwritten annotations and whiteboard drawings are not included in this backup - they remain local to your device only.

Important: Google Drive backup is entirely optional and controlled by you. Your Google account is subject to Google's own privacy policy, not ours. We recommend reviewing it:
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • Local Storage: All study data is stored locally on your device, protected by your device's security features (passcode, biometrics)
  • Encryption: Communication with third-party services (RevenueCat, Google Drive, Firebase, the AI grading service) uses HTTPS encryption
  • No Central Database: We do not maintain a database of user information
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect what's necessary for the app to function
Your Responsibility: Since data is stored on your device, please protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock. If you enable Google Drive backup, secure your Google account with a strong password and two-factor authentication.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

7.1 Local Data

  • Retention: Data remains on your device for as long as you have the app installed
  • Deletion: When you uninstall the app, all local data is automatically deleted by your device's operating system
  • In-App Deletion: The app's Settings screen also lets you delete downloaded files or reset your progress without uninstalling

7.2 Google Drive Backup Data

  • Control: You have full control over the backup file in your Google Drive
  • Deletion: Turning off Google Drive backup in Settings, or revoking the app's access from your Google account, removes our access to that data

7.3 Subscription Data

Subscription and purchase data is retained by RevenueCat according to their own data retention policy.

8. Content Protection

To help protect the educational content in the app from unauthorized copying and redistribution, Al-Taif detects and blocks screenshots, and restricts screen recording or screen sharing, throughout the app (not only on lesson-content screens), using standard operating system features (on iOS and Android).

  • This detection happens entirely on your device
  • No additional personal data is collected, stored, or sent anywhere as part of this feature - it only controls what your device is allowed to capture on screen
  • On Android versions before 15, this also blocks legitimate screen sharing (for example, during a video call) while the app is open, since older Android has no way to distinguish a screen share from a screenshot. On Android 15+ and on iOS, the block is automatically lifted for the duration of a genuine screen share or recording session, and restored once it ends; see our Terms of Service for details

9. Students and Minors

Al-Taif is designed for Grade 11 and Grade 12 students, most of whom are teenagers. We've kept this in mind when designing what data the app collects:

  • No name, photo, or other personal identifier is ever collected
  • All study data (progress, bookmarks, answers) stays on the student's own device unless they choose to enable Google Drive backup
  • The app shows no advertising and does not build behavioral or marketing profiles of any user, including students
  • Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, which have their own account and parental-control safeguards - we recommend a parent or guardian be involved in any purchase

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about what the app collects about your child's use of it, please contact us at info@smarthorizon.co.

10. Your Rights and Choices

10.1 Data Access

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have direct access to your study data through the app interface at any time.

10.2 Data Deletion

  • Local data: Use the Settings screen to delete downloaded files or reset your progress, or uninstall the app to delete everything
  • Google Drive backup: Turn off backup in Settings, or delete the backup file from your Google Drive directly

10.3 Opt-Out Options

  • AI-assisted grading: Simply don't use this feature - compare your answers against the official model answer instead
  • Google Drive backup: Simply don't enable this feature
  • Notifications: Disable the daily study reminder in Settings, or in your device's notification settings
