2. Description of Service

2.1 What the App Does

Al-Taif is a mobile application that helps Grade 11 and Grade 12 students in Oman study physics, including:

  • Lesson explanations (PDF) and instructional videos
  • Practice questions and formula references
  • Interactive whiteboard and annotation tools for working through problems
  • Practice exam simulations
  • Optional AI-assisted feedback on written answers (planned - not yet enabled; currently falls back to comparing against the official model answer)
