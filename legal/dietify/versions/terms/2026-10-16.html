2. What Dietify does

3. Important: your health

Dietify is an assistive tool. It is not medical advice, and it cannot guarantee that a recipe is safe for you. Recipes are "adapted for" your diet, never "guaranteed".

  • Always read product labels, especially for allergies and medical diets.
  • Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet for a medical reason.
  • Every adapted recipe is checked again by safety rules that are not AI, and Dietify flags what it is unsure about, but it can still miss things.
  • Nutrition comes from public food databases, or from AI estimates marked "≈". These values are guides, not measurements.
