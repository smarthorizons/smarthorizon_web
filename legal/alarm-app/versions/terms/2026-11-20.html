2. Description of Service

2.1 What the App Does

AlarmSpark helps you wake up on time and actually get up. Its features include:

  • Alarms with repeat days, sounds (including sounds you import), snooze, groups and holiday pause
  • Wake-up missions that must be completed to stop an alarm: math, memory, typing, captcha, sequence, analog clock, puzzle, shake, steps, squats, photo and barcode
  • Wake-up check, smart wake, quick alarms and alarm templates
  • A morning briefing with your routine and the weather
  • Sleep sounds with a timer, bedtime reminders and a sleep log, with optional Apple Health or Health Connect
  • Statistics, streaks and achievements
  • Home-screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Control Centre and quick settings tiles
  • Apple Watch and Wear OS apps
  • Backup files you can save and restore

2.2 Free and Pro

Free: Alarms and their core features, the math, typing and shake missions, the first alarm sound and the first sleep sound in each category, the sleep log, statistics, widgets, the watch apps and backups.

AlarmSpark Pro: Adds the other missions (memory, captcha, sequence, analog clock, puzzle, steps, squats, photo and barcode), the wake-up check, smart wake, templates that use Pro features, the weather in the morning briefing, the rest of the alarm sounds and sleep sounds, and importing your own alarm sounds.
