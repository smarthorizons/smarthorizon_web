Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Privacy at a Glance:
  • ✅ No accounts - your alarms, history and settings stay on your device
  • ✅ We don't know who you are - no name, email or phone number, unless you choose to contact us

1. Introduction

AlarmSpark is an alarm clock app developed by SmartHorizon ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Muscat, Oman. This Privacy Policy covers the AlarmSpark apps for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS, including their widgets and shortcuts.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What information the app handles, and what stays on your device
  • What little reaches us or the services we use, and why
  • How long it is kept
  • Your rights and choices, including how to turn data sharing off

SmartHorizon is the controller of the limited personal data described here. By using AlarmSpark, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information the App Handles

Key Point: AlarmSpark has no user accounts. Everything you create in the app is stored on your device. We receive only anonymous usage data and crash reports (which you can turn off), subscription records needed to unlock Pro, and any message you choose to send us.

2.1 What We Never Collect

  • ❌ Name, email address or phone number (unless you send us a message, see 2.10)
  • ❌ User accounts or passwords
  • ❌ Contacts, messages or social media profiles
  • ❌ Precise location
  • ❌ Advertising identifiers (the app does not use the IDFA or Android advertising ID, and does not ask permission to track you)

2.2 Stored Only on Your Device

  • Alarms: time, label, repeat days, sounds, volume, snooze, missions and their settings, groups
  • Your morning routine items and sleep goal
  • Wake-up history, sleep log, statistics and achievements
  • Your sleep sound mix and any alarm sounds you import
  • App settings and preferences

If you use the Apple Watch or Wear OS app, your phone sends the alarm details it needs directly to your watch (through Apple's or Google's watch connection). This does not pass through us.

2.3 Location (Optional)

When: Only when you turn on weather in the morning briefing (a Pro feature) and allow location access

What: Your approximate location (the app requests low accuracy only)

How: Each time the briefing fetches the weather, the approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, a weather service, which returns the forecast

Storage: We do not receive or store your location, and it is never included in analytics

Control: Turn weather off in the app, or deny location access in your device settings

2.4 Camera: Photo and Barcode Missions (Optional)

Photo mission:

  • You take a reference photo of an object (for example, your coffee mug)
  • Your device recognises what is in the photo on the device itself (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android)
  • Only the resulting labels (such as "cup") are saved with the alarm
  • When the alarm rings, you photograph the object again and the device compares the labels
  • Photos are never uploaded. The picture itself is a temporary file that your device clears

Barcode mission: The value of the barcode you scan is saved with the alarm on your device so the app can compare it when the alarm rings. It is never uploaded.

Control: Deny camera access, or don't use these missions.

2.5 Motion (Optional)

Shake, steps and squats missions count your movement with your device's motion sensors. Smart wake on Apple Watch uses wrist movement. This is processed on your devices and never uploaded.

2.6 Apple Health and Health Connect (Optional)

If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), AlarmSpark can read and write sleep sessions - to show your sleep history and to save the nights you log in the app.

  • Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health or Health Connect
  • It is never sent to us or to any third party
  • It is never used for advertising, marketing or analytics, and never sold
  • Analytics only records that you connected (yes or no) and roughly how many nights were imported (a range such as "4-6"), never any health values
  • You can disconnect at any time in the Health app or in Health Connect

2.7 Sleep Sound Downloads

Some sleep sounds are built into the app. The others are downloaded from our storage at Cloudflare when you choose them. As with any download, Cloudflare receives your device's IP address and basic request details in its standard server logs. We do not use these to identify you. Downloaded sounds are kept in the app's cache, which your device may clear.

2.8 Usage Data and Crash Reports (You Can Turn These Off)

We use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring (by Google) to find bugs, keep alarms reliable and learn which features are useful. These collect:

  • App version, device model, operating system version and language
  • General region, such as country (derived by Google from your IP address)
  • Which screens are opened
  • Feature events, for example:
    • An alarm was saved (number of missions, mission types, the hour it is set for, and whether it has a label - never the label itself)
    • An alarm rang, was snoozed or dismissed; a mission started, was completed or failed
    • Sleep sounds played or downloaded; a setting changed (which setting and the option chosen)
    • A backup was saved or restored; the Pro screen was shown and how a purchase attempt ended
  • A few general properties: app language, whether you have Pro, number of alarms (as a range), theme and clock format
  • Crash and error reports, with the app's last few steps before the problem
  • Performance timings, such as app start-up time, the time from an alarm firing to its screen appearing, and download times

Never collected: alarm labels, anything you type, photos, barcode values, your location, health values or your routine items.

Identifier: Firebase uses a random identifier for your app installation. It is not linked to your name or any account, and we cannot identify you from it.

Your switch: In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & terms → Share anonymous usage data. It is on by default. Turning it off immediately stops analytics, crash reports and performance monitoring, deletes crash reports not yet sent, and resets the analytics identifier. Restoring a backup never turns it back on.

2.9 Subscriptions and Purchases

We use RevenueCat to manage AlarmSpark Pro subscriptions. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID (generated by RevenueCat)
  • Your purchases and subscription status (product, price, currency, renewal and expiry)
  • Store country, device type, operating system and app version

Payment Information: Payments are processed only by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We and RevenueCat never see your card or billing details.

Purchase analytics: While "Share anonymous usage data" is on, RevenueCat also reports subscription events (purchases, trials, renewals and cancellations, with price and currency) to our Google Analytics, linked to the app's analytics identifier rather than to you. Turning the switch off removes that link.

Purpose: To unlock Pro, restore purchases, and understand subscription revenue.

2.10 Contacting Us (Optional)

If you use Settings → Contact us, the app sends us:

  • The message type, your message, and the name and email address you enter
  • Any screenshots you choose to attach
  • App version, platform, operating system version, device model and app language, to help us understand the problem

The message passes through our Cloudflare relay and is delivered by Resend as an email to our support inbox (Google Workspace). No identifier for your device or installation is included. If you email us directly instead, we receive what you put in that email.

Purpose: To answer you and fix what you report.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) applies, we rely on the following:

Processing Legal Basis
Subscriptions and purchases (RevenueCat, App Store, Google Play) Performance of a contract - needed to sell, restore and provide Pro (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b))
Usage data, crash reports, performance data and purchase analytics Our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable and improving it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). It is on by default; you can object at any time with the in-app switch, which stops it immediately
Location, camera, motion and health access Your permission, given through your device's prompt; you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings. This data is processed on your device (location is only passed to the weather service)
Messages you send us Your request - needed to answer you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in fixing reported problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))
Legal requests Compliance with a legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c))

We have carried out a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) for usage data and crash reports. It concluded that the impact on your privacy is low, because the data contains no direct identifiers, is limited to what we need, is not used for advertising or profiling, and can be switched off in the app at any time.

5. Backup

You can save a backup file of your alarms, missions, groups, routine, settings, wake-up history, sleep log and sleep sound mix, and restore it later or on a new device. Sleep imported from Apple Health or Health Connect is never put in a backup file; it stays in Health.

  • How it works: The app creates the file and opens your device's share sheet. You choose where to keep it - for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email
  • Restore: You pick the file in the app
  • No sign-in: The app does not sign in to any cloud account and does not upload the file anywhere itself
  • We never receive it
Important: Backup files are not encrypted by the app and include your alarm labels, routine items, wake-up history and sleep log. Anyone with the file can read it, so keep it somewhere private. Wherever you store it, that service's own privacy policy applies, for example Apple's or Google's.

7. Data Security

  • On your device: Your data is protected by your device's security (passcode, Face ID, fingerprint)
  • In transit: Connections to Firebase, RevenueCat, Open-Meteo, Cloudflare and Resend use HTTPS encryption
  • No central database: We do not run a database of user information
  • Minimal data: We only collect what is described in this policy
Your Responsibility: Please protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock, and keep backup files somewhere private, since they are not encrypted by the app.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 On Your Device

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app (for example, deleting an alarm) or uninstall the app, which removes it
  • Downloaded sleep sounds are kept in the app's cache, which your device may clear at any time

8.2 Backup Files

Backup files are kept wherever you saved them, for as long as you choose. Delete them there.

8.3 Usage Data, Crash Reports and Subscriptions

  • Crash reports: Kept by Crashlytics for about 90 days
  • Analytics events: Kept for no longer than 14 months, then deleted. Aggregated reports that cannot identify anyone may be kept longer
  • Subscription records: Kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and tax obligations, under RevenueCat's policy
  • Messages you send us: Kept in our support inbox for up to 2 years after the conversation ends, then deleted. Resend keeps delivery records under its own policy. Ask us to delete your messages at any time
  • Weather and download requests: Open-Meteo and Cloudflare keep standard request logs under their own policies
  • Resetting: Turning off "Share anonymous usage data" resets the analytics identifier, so earlier data can no longer be connected to your installation

9. Children's Privacy

AlarmSpark is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

  • The app has no accounts and asks for a name or email only if someone chooses to send us a message
  • Everything created in the app stays on the device

If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, please contact us at alarmspark.support@smarthorizon.co and we will delete it.

10.2 GDPR Rights (European Economic Area and UK)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data
  • Data portability (use the backup file)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest - use the in-app switch, which takes effect immediately

10.3 Oman Personal Data Protection Law

Under Oman's Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022), you have the right to know how your data is processed, to access, correct, transfer and delete it, to withdraw your consent, and to complain to the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology.

10.4 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect (see Section 2)
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Know that we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising

We do not discriminate against users who exercise their rights.

10.5 How to Make a Request

Email alarmspark.support@smarthorizon.co. Because we have no accounts and analytics uses only a random installation identifier, we usually cannot link analytics data to a particular person. The in-app switch is the quickest way to stop collection and reset that identifier.
