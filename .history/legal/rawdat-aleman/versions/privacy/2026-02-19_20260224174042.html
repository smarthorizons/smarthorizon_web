1. Introduction

Welcome to Rawdat Aleman ("App"), developed by Smart Horizon ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with Islamic content and spiritual resources.

Rawdat Aleman is a comprehensive Islamic content application that provides daily azkar (remembrances), Quranic verses, duas (supplications), digital tasbeeh counter, and notification reminders for daily spiritual practices.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at admin@smarthorizon.co.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using Rawdat Aleman, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. What Information Do We Collect?

Key Point: Rawdat Aleman does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information that identifies you. All your preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We do NOT collect: name, email address, phone number, user accounts, passwords, location data, camera access, or photos.

The following is stored locally on your device only:

  • Azkar reading preferences and favorites
  • Tasbeeh counter statistics
  • Bookmarked Quranic verses and duas
  • Notification preferences and reminder times
  • App display settings (language, theme, font size)

2.2 Analytics Information

We use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to maintain app stability and improve reliability. Firebase anonymously collects:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Device language and region (country-level)
  • App usage events (screens visited, features used)
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Crash reports and technical diagnostics

Firebase assigns an anonymous identifier to your device. We cannot identify you personally from this data. We do not track which specific azkar you read, which Quranic verses you view, or your spiritual practice habits.

Legal Basis: Analytics processing is based on our Legitimate Interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). See Section 3 for full justification and your right to object.

2.3 Notification Data

All notifications are handled entirely locally on your device — no third-party notification services are used. You can customize notification times for daily azkar reminders. Notification settings are stored only on your device. We do not track whether you interact with or dismiss notifications.

3. Legal Basis for Analytics Processing (GDPR)

We process anonymous analytics data based on our Legitimate Interest under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. We have conducted a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) and concluded the following:

Our Interest: Maintaining a stable and reliable Islamic content platform that users depend on for daily spiritual practice. Analytics helps us detect crashes, fix bugs, and ensure the app works reliably across thousands of device and OS combinations.

Impact on Users: Minimal — data is anonymous and aggregated, no personal identifiers are collected, no specific spiritual content or practices are tracked, and data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Your Right to Object (GDPR Article 21): You may object to analytics processing using your device's OS-level controls:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Turn off "Share iPhone Analytics" and "Share With App Developers"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy → Usage & Diagnostics → Turn off

These settings affect all apps on your device. This is the appropriate method for objecting to legitimate interest-based analytics under GDPR.

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Local Storage

All your azkar preferences, tasbeeh counts, bookmarks, and settings are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage area — protected by your device's operating system and accessible only by Rawdat Aleman. No data is stored on our servers.

6.2 Security Measures

Communication with Firebase services uses HTTPS encryption. We do not maintain any central database of user information. All personal preferences and spiritual content remain on your device.

Your Responsibility: Since all data is stored on your device, please secure it with a strong passcode or biometric lock and keep your OS updated with security patches.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

7.1 Local Data

Retention: Data remains on your device for as long as the app is installed.

Deletion: Uninstalling the app automatically deletes all local data. On Android, you can also clear app data via Settings → Apps → Rawdat Aleman → Storage → Clear Data.

7.2 Analytics Data

Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase according to their data retention policy (typically 2–14 months), after which it is aggregated and anonymized permanently. Because this data is anonymous, we cannot identify or delete data belonging to a specific user.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Data Access

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have direct access through the app interface. You can view your preferences, bookmarks, and settings at any time.

8.2 Data Deletion

  • Local data: Uninstall the app to delete all local data immediately
  • Analytics data: Cannot be individually deleted as it's anonymous and aggregated; future collection can be prevented via OS-level analytics controls

8.3 Analytics Opt-Out

You may object to analytics processing using your device OS settings as described in Section 3. This is the appropriate method under GDPR Article 21 for objecting to legitimate interest-based processing.

8.4 Notification Control

You can disable or customize notifications:

  • iOS: Settings → Notifications → Rawdat Aleman
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Rawdat Aleman → Notifications
  • In-app: App Settings → Notifications → Customize times or disable

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data, restrict or object to processing, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us at admin@smarthorizon.co — we will respond within 30 days.

8.6 CCPA Rights (California Users)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect (Section 2), request deletion (uninstall the app), and opt-out of the sale of personal information — which does not apply as we do not sell data. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
